  accTitle: Writing a paper with AcademicPaperWriter
  accDescr: A paper repository is set up, then either a new idea is developed and drafted or an existing draft is imported; the manuscript is refined, verified, and cited in a loop until ready, then finalized and exported.
  S(["Start a new paper"]):::start --> P1("Set up the paper repository ⤵"):::process
  P1 --> D1{"Already have a draft?"}:::decision
  D1 -- "No" --> P2("Develop and scope the research idea"):::process
  P2 --> P3("Write the first full draft"):::process
  D1 -- "Yes" --> P4("Import the existing draft"):::process
  P3 --> P5("Refine, verify, and cite"):::process
  P4 --> P5
  P5 --> D2{"Ready to finalize?"}:::decision
  D2 -- "Keep refining" --> P5
  D2 -- "Ready" --> P6("Finalize and export for co-authors"):::process
  P6 --> Z(["Paper ready"]):::success
