# Interview loop

The shared grilling loop. `grill-me` is this loop and nothing else; `grill-with-docs` reaches this same file via `../grill-me/interview-loop.md` and adds a domain-model layer on top. Edit the loop here and both skills inherit it — this is its single source of truth.

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of the plan. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask questions one at a time, waiting for my answer before continuing.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead of asking.

Keep going until every decision the interview has surfaced has a recorded answer and no answer still rests on an unresolved one — not until it merely feels resolved. When the tree is exhausted by that test, say so and stop.
