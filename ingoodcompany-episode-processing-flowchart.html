  click P1 call nodeDetail() "Reads the podcast feed, saves episode metadata, and downloads the audio file. Already-downloaded episodes are skipped."
  click P2 call nodeDetail() "Speech-to-text with word-level timestamps, plus separating who is speaking. The slowest stage — checkpointed so failures never redo finished work."
  click P3 call nodeDetail() "Turns anonymous speaker tracks into host and guest using three escalating tiers: jingle stripping, scoring heuristics, and an LLM fallback."
  click P4 call nodeDetail() "Mechanically splits the labeled conversation into question-answer pairs using speaker turns and question detection — no LLM involved."
  click P5 call nodeDetail() "An LLM reads the numbered question list, draws topic boundaries, and gives each group a descriptive label."
  click Z call nodeDetail() "The episode's topics and question-answer pairs land in the project database, ready for analysis and querying."
