Deep revision of a section (refine-heavy)

What happens when you ask the AcademicPaperWriter harness for a full re-evaluation and rewrite of one section — click a node for details, hover for a summary.

flowchart TD
  S(["Start"]):::startEnd --> P1["Load the paper's context"]:::process
  P1 --> P2["Critique the target section"]:::process
  P2 --> D1{"Approve the direction?"}:::decision
  D1 -- "Cancel" --> E0(["Stop: nothing changed"]):::stop
  D1 -- "Approve" --> P3["Rewrite the section"]:::process
  P3 --> P4["Run deep integrity checks"]:::process
  P4 --> D2{"All checks pass?"}:::decision
  D2 -- "No" --> E1(["Stop: review issues, re-run"]):::stop
  D2 -- "Yes" --> P5["Check citations and language"]:::process
  P5 --> Z(["Section refined"]):::startEnd

  classDef stop     fill:#c53030,stroke:#9b2c2c,color:#fff
  classDef process  fill:#3182ce,stroke:#2b6cb0,color:#fff
  classDef decision fill:#dd8a12,stroke:#b7791f,color:#fff

  click P1 call nodeDetail() "Silently reads the paper's rules, constraints, config, and research notes, then confirms which section it is about to work on before doing anything."
  click P2 call nodeDetail() "Produces a structured critique: what works, what is broken, a proposed rewrite direction, and how invasive the rewrite would be."
  click D1 call nodeDetail() "A mandatory human gate. Nothing is rewritten until you explicitly approve the proposed direction — you can also amend it or walk away."
  click P3 call nodeDetail() "Rewrites only the approved section, keeping AI-attribution markers, flagging open questions, and citing only already-validated sources."
  click P4 call nodeDetail() "A three-layer mathematical integrity gate checks the rewritten section, retrying fixes automatically before giving up."
  click D2 call nodeDetail() "If the gate still fails after retries, the run halts with a written report — the rewrite never silently proceeds on broken math."
  click P5 call nodeDetail() "Four parallel checks on citations, cross-references, and AI-sounding language; findings are recorded but do not block."
  click Z call nodeDetail() "You get a one-screen summary, a dated review report, and an automatic diff of exactly what changed."
