  accTitle: Validate and repair the sentence
  accDescr: Score the sentence against known vocabulary. If too many words are unknown, an LLM judge diagnoses the violations and a remediation model rewrites the sentence, looping until it passes, stops improving, or the retry budget runs out.
  S(["↰ From: Flashcard generation"]):::start --> P1("Score sentence against known words ⤵"):::process
  P1 --> D1{"Enough words known?"}:::decision
  D1 -- "Yes" --> Z(["Sentence accepted"]):::success
  D1 -- "No" --> D2{"Keep trying?"}:::decision
  D2 -- "Yes" --> P2("Judge diagnoses each violation"):::process
  P2 --> P3("Rewrite the sentence"):::process
  P3 -- "Re-check" --> P1
  D2 -- "No" --> Z2(["Best attempt kept"]):::success
